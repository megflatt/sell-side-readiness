# Sell-Side Readiness

A readiness assessment, valuation-range estimate, prep checklist, and exit-route finder for business owners considering selling their business.

Static site — open `index.html`. Nothing entered on the page is saved or sent anywhere.

## What it does

1. **Business snapshot** — revenue, SDE / adjusted EBITDA, industry, history, and growth trend.
2. **Readiness score** — how buyers will score the business across six dimensions: clean financials, owner dependence, customer diversification, a team that stays, documented systems, and a believable growth story. Weak spots are flagged explicitly.
3. **Valuation-range estimate** — a rule-of-thumb SDE/EBITDA multiple by size band, adjusted for industry, growth, and readiness score. Planning range only, not a valuation opinion.
4. **Prep checklist** — financial, operational, legal, people, and process items to work before talking price, with a progress bar.
5. **Route finder** — 6 questions that point to a likely exit route: strategic buyer / private equity, individual buyer / search fund, family or management transfer / ESOP, or hold, fix, then sell.
6. **Diligence vault** — the documents to gather before buyers ask for them.

## Disclaimer

Educational only. Not legal, tax, accounting, or financial advice, and not a valuation opinion or broker's price opinion. Multiples and scores are planning rules of thumb — actual price depends on the market, the books, and a buyer's diligence. Consult a CPA, attorney, and qualified M&A advisor before making decisions about selling.
