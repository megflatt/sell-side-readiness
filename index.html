    Size bands buyers talk in (rule of thumb)

    • Under about $500K in earnings: mainly individual buyers. Multiples are lowest and deals often include seller financing.
    • About $500K to $2M in earnings: the search fund and small private equity zone. Cleaner books and a real management layer raise the multiple fast.
    • Over about $2M in earnings: institutional buyers enter. Audited quality financials and a team that stays matter most.

    A note before you act on any of this

    This tool is educational. It is not legal, tax, accounting, or financial advice, and it is not a valuation opinion or a broker's price opinion. Multiples and scores here are planning rules of thumb drawn from how small business transactions are commonly discussed. Your actual price will depend on your market, your books, and a buyer's diligence. Talk to your CPA, attorney, and (when the time comes) a qualified M&A advisor before making decisions about selling.
